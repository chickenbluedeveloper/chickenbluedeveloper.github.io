About the organizer

Who we are

Chickenblue believes there are many great writers in the world who are not published — and plenty of published fiction that frankly should never have made it that far.

The ambition is to build a credible space for new voices where they can get their first leg up. Winners are chosen on the strength of their work, not the size of their social media following.

Questions? Reach us at hello@chickenblue.com
