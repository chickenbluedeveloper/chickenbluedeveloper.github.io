Submission guidelines

How to enter

01

Word count: Your story must be between 100 and 1000 words. Titles are not included in the count.

02

Format: Paste your story directly into the submission form. Use plain text only.

03

Originality: Work must be your own, previously unpublished, and not under consideration elsewhere.

04

Limit per person: One submission per author. Multiple entries will be disqualified.

05

Rights: Authors retain full copyright. We ask for first publication rights for winning and shortlisted entries only.

About the organizer

Who we are

We're a small collective of readers, editors, and writers who believe that brevity is its own kind of art. Flash fiction forces a kind of honesty — there's no room to hide, no space for filler. Only what matters.

This contest is our way of celebrating that discipline, and of finding voices we haven't heard yet. Whether you've been writing for decades or just discovered flash last week, we want to read your story.

Questions? Reach us at hello@chickenblue.com
